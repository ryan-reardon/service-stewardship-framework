# Innovation & Modernization Playbook

## Engineering Manager Guidance

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Domain Purpose

Ensure the service evolves deliberately, replacing aging technology and practices before they become a source of risk, and that experimentation capacity is protected rather than perpetually deferred.

Innovation & Modernization is the discipline of treating technical evolution as planned, funded work rather than crisis response. Services that never modernize eventually can't, because the cost and risk of change grow faster than the appetite to fund it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. What Success Looks Like (Level 3-4 Target State)

A service with mature innovation and modernization practice demonstrates:

- Modernization capacity explicitly protected in planning, tied to the run/grow/modernize allocation from Strategic Stewardship
- Legacy and deprecated components identified with concrete retirement or replacement plans
- Dedicated capacity and a lightweight process for experimentation (spikes, proofs of concept, evaluation of new approaches)
- Modernization initiatives prioritized by risk and value, not simply by age
- A technology radar, or equivalent, tracking what to adopt, trial, hold, or retire
- Modernization progress tracked and communicated, rather than happening silently in the background

At Level 4 maturity:

- Modernization needs are predicted, with aging risk modeled before it becomes urgent
- Experimentation outcomes systematically feed the roadmap instead of dead-ending after the proof of concept
- Platform evolution decisions are informed by ongoing industry and technology trend analysis

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Engineering Manager Responsibilities

- Maintain visibility into aging and legacy components and their risk profile
- Protect modernization and experimentation capacity from being consumed by delivery pressure
- Prioritize modernization initiatives using explicit risk and value criteria
- Establish a lightweight process for evaluating and adopting new tools or approaches
- Track and communicate modernization progress to stakeholders
- Partner with Architectural Integrity on debt-driven modernization priorities
- Avoid both extremes: chasing every new technology, and never modernizing at all

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Governance Model & Cadence

**Weekly:**
- Track active modernization and experimentation work in progress

**Monthly:**
- Review the modernization backlog and capacity allocation
- Review experimentation outcomes

**Quarterly:**
- Conduct a formal modernization roadmap review
- Refresh the technology radar

**Annually:**
- Conduct strategic modernization planning tied to the Strategic Stewardship lifecycle review

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Required Artifacts

- Legacy and aging component inventory with risk ratings
- Modernization backlog with documented prioritization criteria
- Technology radar (adopt/trial/hold/retire)
- Experimentation and proof-of-concept log with outcomes
- Modernization capacity allocation record
- Modernization progress report

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. Operating Mechanisms

- Modernization capacity guardrail: an explicit percentage or time allocation protected from delivery pressure
- Risk-based prioritization rubric for modernization initiatives
- Lightweight experimentation process using time-boxed spikes or proofs of concept with defined evaluation criteria
- Technology radar review process
- Regular modernization progress reporting

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Common Failure Modes (Anti-Patterns)

- Modernization always loses to feature delivery pressure
- Legacy components ignored until they cause an outage or block a feature
- Experimentation happens informally and invisibly, with no protected capacity
- New technology adopted based on individual preference rather than evaluation criteria
- Modernization work invisible to stakeholders and dismissed as gold-plating
- No clear retirement plan for deprecated components, so they linger indefinitely

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. Maturity Advancement Roadmap

Level 1 → Reactive:
- Inventory legacy and aging components
- Identify the highest-risk modernization candidates
- Establish minimal experimentation capacity

Level 2 → Managed:
- Protect a modernization capacity allocation
- Establish prioritization criteria for the modernization backlog
- Create an initial technology radar

Level 3 → Optimized:
- Formalize the experimentation process with evaluation criteria
- Track and report modernization progress regularly
- Tie modernization priorities to architectural debt and strategic goals

Level 4 → Predictive & Autonomous:
- Predict modernization needs before they become urgent
- Systematically feed experimentation outcomes into the roadmap
- Inform platform evolution decisions with industry and trend analysis

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 9. Monthly Metrics Dashboard

- Modernization capacity allocated vs. planned
- Legacy and aging component count and risk trend
- Modernization backlog age and throughput
- Experimentation and proof-of-concept count and outcomes (adopted vs. rejected)
- Technology radar changes
- Modernization progress vs. roadmap

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10. 90-Day Action Plan for a New Manager

**Days 1-30:**
- Inventory legacy and aging components and assess risk
- Review current modernization capacity allocation, if any exists
- Review experimentation practices and available capacity
- Identify the highest-risk modernization candidates
- Assess any existing technology radar or equivalent

**Days 31-60:**
- Establish or protect a modernization capacity allocation
- Build prioritization criteria for the modernization backlog
- Launch a lightweight experimentation process
- Draft or refresh the technology radar
- Begin tracking modernization progress

**Days 61-90:**
- Publish a modernization roadmap and progress report
- Establish a quarterly technology radar review
- Set measurable targets for modernization capacity and legacy risk reduction
- Connect modernization priorities to the Architectural Integrity debt register
- Institutionalize the experimentation-to-roadmap feedback loop
