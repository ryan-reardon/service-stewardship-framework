# Financial Stewardship Assessment

This assessment evaluates the maturity of Financial Stewardship within a technology service.

The objective is cost discipline integrity, not budget compliance theater.

Score conservatively.  
Require evidence.  
Default to the lower level when uncertain.  

---

# Domain Purpose

Financial Stewardship ensures the service maintains transparent, defensible cost management and that investment remains justified by the value it delivers.

Cost visibility is not cost governance.
Financial discipline is measured by optimization action, not reporting frequency.

---

# Level 1: Reactive

Cost is opaque and financial decisions are made without a clear model.

Characteristics:

- TCO not tracked or understood across categories
- No cost-to-value model exists
- Budget variance reported but not explained
- Vendor and licensing contracts managed informally, with renewals often unreviewed
- Cost optimization reactive to budget pressure, not continuous
- New initiatives approved without cost impact assessment

Evidence Review:

- Is TCO tracked by category (infrastructure, licensing, labor, vendor)?
- Does a cost-to-value model exist?
- Is budget variance documented with explanations?
- Are vendor contracts inventoried with renewal dates and utilization data?
- Is cost optimization maintained as a backlog item?

If most answers are "no," score Level 1.

---

# Level 2: Managed

Basic cost visibility exists but optimization and attribution are limited.

Characteristics:

- TCO tracked at a high level with major cost drivers identified
- Initial cost-to-value model drafted, even if rough
- Budget variance reviewed monthly with some documented explanations
- Vendor and licensing contract inventory established with renewal dates
- Cost optimization work occurring but not maintained as a continuous backlog
- Some initiatives include cost impact assessment in business cases

Evidence Review:

- TCO breakdown by category available and reviewed on cadence
- Cost-to-value model exists and is used in investment discussions
- Budget variance documented with explanations for major variances
- Contract inventory current with upcoming renewals flagged
- At least one cost optimization initiative completed in the past quarter

If cost visibility exists but does not consistently drive optimization or inform investment decisions, score Level 2.

---

# Level 3: Optimized

Cost is continuously governed, optimization is disciplined, and investment decisions include financial rigor.

Characteristics:

- TCO tracked by category with cost tagging and attribution to service and initiative level
- Cost-to-value model current and used in roadmap and investment decisions
- Budget variance reviewed monthly with documented explanations and trend analysis
- Vendor and licensing utilization reviewed ahead of renewals with negotiation actions taken
- Cost optimization maintained as a continuous backlog with realized savings tracked
- Business case requirement enforced for new initiatives above a defined threshold
- Cost anomaly alerting in place with investigation process

Evidence Review:

- Cost attribution documented at service and initiative level
- Cost-to-value ratio reviewed monthly with trend data
- Vendor utilization reviews completed ahead of major renewals
- Cost optimization backlog maintained with completed items and savings tracked
- Business case documentation includes cost impact for recent approved initiatives
- Cost anomaly alerts configured and reviewed

If financial governance measurably reduces waste and keeps investment defensible, score Level 3.

---

# Level 4: Proactive

Financial posture is forecasted, unit economics are tracked, and cost informs strategic planning.

Characteristics:

- Cost forecasting under growth and contraction scenarios in active use
- Unit economics tracked and actively improved over time
- Financial posture integrated into strategic roadmap planning
- Vendor contract negotiations data-driven with utilization and market benchmark inputs
- Cost optimization continuous and measured with cumulative savings tracked against targets

Evidence Review:

- Scenario-based cost forecasts present and referenced in planning discussions
- Unit cost trend tracked and improvement initiatives active
- Financial posture summary integrated into lifecycle and strategic reviews
- Vendor negotiation outcomes documented with financial impact
- Cumulative optimization savings tracked against annual targets

If financial decisions are anticipatory and cost is continuously connected to strategic investment decisions, score Level 4.

---

# Scoring Summary

| Level | Posture |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | Opaque and reactive |
| 2 | Visible but inconsistently governed |
| 3 | Continuously governed and optimization-disciplined |
| 4 | Predictive and strategically integrated |

---

# Risk Considerations

Financial Stewardship weaknesses often manifest as:

- Cost surprises during budget reviews that no one can explain
- Vendor contracts auto-renewing for unused or underused tools
- Cost growth outpacing delivered value without anyone noticing
- Investment decisions approved without financial modeling
- Cost optimization attempted only during budget crises rather than continuously
- Engineering leaders unable to articulate why the service costs what it costs

Low maturity in this domain creates investment credibility risk and compounds across Strategic Stewardship and Innovation & Modernization.

Financial debt accumulates silently and surfaces as budget overruns and indefensible investment narratives.

---

# Target Level & Improvement Plan

After scoring:

1. Document current level.
2. Define target level aligned to the service's investment scale and organizational financial governance requirements.
3. Identify the cost visibility and optimization gaps with greatest financial impact.
4. Assign accountable owner.
5. Define timeline for reassessment.

Reassess quarterly during active improvement periods.

---

# Final Reminder

A service can stay within budget while being financially undisciplined. Budget compliance is not cost governance. Financial Stewardship determines whether the investment in the service remains defensible over time. Score honestly.
