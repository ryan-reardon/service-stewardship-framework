# Service Experience Assessment

This assessment evaluates the maturity of Service Experience within a technology service.

The objective is customer trust integrity, not internal process optimism.

Score conservatively.
Require evidence.
Default to the lower level when uncertain.

---

# Domain Purpose

Service Experience ensures the service delivers a consistently positive experience for the people who use it, that expectations are clearly set and met, and that feedback actively shapes the roadmap.

A service can be reliable by every internal metric while failing its users.
Experience is what customers remember. Performance is the baseline they expect.

---

# Level 1: Reactive

Experience is undefined and satisfaction is unmeasured.

Characteristics:

- Customer and user segments not clearly defined
- No satisfaction measurement in place
- Support ticket intake informal or inconsistent
- Documentation absent or severely outdated
- Feedback collected, if at all, but not connected to decisions
- Incident communication ad hoc and inconsistent

Evidence Review:

- Are customer and user segments documented with explicit expectations?
- Is satisfaction measured through CSAT, NPS, or equivalent?
- Is support ticket intake structured with root-cause tracking?
- Is documentation current and maintained on a defined cadence?
- Is feedback visibly connected to roadmap decisions?

If most answers are "no," score Level 1.

---

# Level 2: Managed

Basic experience structure exists but consistency and measurement are limited.

Characteristics:

- Customer and user segments identified, though expectations may be informal
- Basic satisfaction measurement introduced, even if tracking is inconsistent
- Support ticket intake structured with initial categorization
- Documentation exists for primary use cases, though gaps and staleness are present
- Feedback collected but not systematically connected to roadmap
- Incident communication templates exist but are not consistently applied

Evidence Review:

- Customer and user segment definitions exist
- Satisfaction metric tracked, even if infrequently reviewed
- Support ticket categorization and root-cause tracking in place for major issues
- Documentation covers primary workflows
- At least some feedback has visibly influenced roadmap decisions

If experience structure exists but does not consistently improve satisfaction or reduce friction, score Level 2.

---

# Level 3: Optimized

Experience is measured, governed, and producing visible improvement.

Characteristics:

- Customer and user segments well defined with documented expectations and journey map
- Satisfaction measured consistently and reviewed on a monthly cadence
- Support and success functions operating proactively, not only in response to escalations
- Documentation and self-service maintained as the service changes with freshness tracked
- Feedback loops directly connected to roadmap prioritization with visible evidence
- Communication protocols defined and consistently applied for incidents and major changes
- Root-cause tracking for recurring issues connected to Engineering and Reliability backlogs

Evidence Review:

- Satisfaction dashboard reviewed monthly with trend data available
- Support ticket root-cause distribution tracked and decreasing for recurring issues
- Self-service deflection rate measured and improving
- Documentation review cadence tied to release cycles
- Feedback-to-roadmap conversion rate tracked
- Communication templates applied consistently with post-incident reviews

If experience governance measurably improves satisfaction and reduces recurring friction, score Level 3.

---

# Level 4: Proactive

Experience is predictive, segmented, and continuously self-improving.

Characteristics:

- Satisfaction and churn risk predicted from usage and support signals before appearing in volume
- Experience segmented and personalized based on how different user groups actually use the service
- Self-service improving continuously through usage analytics rather than periodic redesign
- Feedback loops automated and feeding structured roadmap inputs with minimal manual effort
- Documentation quality measured and maintained at high coverage through automated tooling

Evidence Review:

- Predictive satisfaction or churn risk signals in active use
- User segment-specific experience data reviewed in governance cadence
- Self-service deflection improving on a continuous measurable trend
- Feedback-to-roadmap conversion rate high and consistently tracked
- Documentation coverage and freshness measured automatically

If experience decisions are anticipatory and satisfaction is continuously improving, score Level 4.

---

# Scoring Summary

| Level | Posture |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | Undefined and unmeasured |
| 2 | Structured but inconsistently applied |
| 3 | Measured and continuously improving |
| 4 | Predictive and self-improving |

---

# Risk Considerations

Service Experience weaknesses often manifest as:

- Customers churning or escalating for reasons engineering was never aware of
- Support volume consuming engineering capacity invisibly, without appearing in planning
- Documentation debt causing repeated questions and friction that could have been avoided
- Roadmap decisions made without knowledge of what users are actually experiencing
- Trust eroded during incidents by communication that is slow, inconsistent, or absent

Low maturity in this domain creates customer trust risk that technical excellence alone cannot recover.

Experience dysfunction is often invisible inside the engineering team and highly visible to every customer who encounters it.

---

# Target Level & Improvement Plan

After scoring:

1. Document current level.
2. Define target level aligned to customer segment expectations and service criticality.
3. Identify the experience gaps with greatest customer trust impact.
4. Assign accountable owner.
5. Define timeline for reassessment.

Reassess quarterly during active improvement periods.

---

# Final Reminder

Customers do not experience architecture or internal SLOs. They experience documentation, communication, support responsiveness, and whether anyone acted on what they said. Score honestly.
